How it works — charge and rebate

The service charges its normal $0.05 USDC x402 challenge. After your payment settles on Base, the RAEN rebater sends the $0.05 straight back to your wallet. Net cost: $0.00.

Why not just a free endpoint? Because the point is to prove the real payment path — you experience the actual x402 402-challenge → EIP-3009 settlement → USDC rebate loop, end to end. Free tier fakes it; this doesn't.

Steps

1
Have a wallet with a little ETH for gas on Base (the settlement is an on-chain EIP-3009 transfer — even though the payment is rebated, the gas is yours).
2
POST to the law-lookup endpoint with your address in the x-payer-address header. You'll get an HTTP 402 with a payment challenge.
3
Pay the challenge with any x402 client (or the RAEN cookbook recipes).
4
Within seconds of settlement, the rebater pays $0.05 USDC back to your address. Check it on Basescan.
One free call per wallet per 30 days. The rebate is keyed to settled payments — an unpaid challenge gets no rebate. The rebate treasury (AgentCash) is capped at 100 rebates/day; if it's exhausted, the call simply isn't rebated that day.

Copy-paste

curl -s -X POST https://royal-ruby-x402.fly.dev/api/law-lookup \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -H 'x-payer-address: 0xYOUR_WALLET' \
  -d '{"question":"How long does a credit bureau have to investigate a dispute?"}'

The 402 response's payment-required header carries a base64 challenge — decode it and you'll see payTo: 0x9b8a27e0…, network: eip155:8453, scheme: exact. Pay it with the x402 client from the cookbook and your answer JSON comes back.
